# Playtest 2026-11-10 — The Hill v0.1.0

Source: typed (Telegram). Transcribed by Gary. Kaden's words quoted verbatim; personal lines left out.

## Who played
Kaden (and family). All three games played; The Hill got the detailed notes ("The Hill is the one I'm most excited about ... I'm just gonna give everything that I saw.").

## What he asked for (verbatim, grouped)

### Story and intro
> "I would love more of a story when we start up the game. Something telling us that we got the hill as an inheritance is ours to do as we want with it, be careful of the mineshaft. There's crazy stuff that happens down there. Something like that."

> "I think we have some sort of intro scene where somebody explains that to us. I think that's the best way to do it."

### NPC arcs and cutscenes
> "The story for the NPCs is also very weak, the mole stole my shovel, but I was still digging, and then they just gave it right back. I would much rather space it out where the mole does not show itself right away. Maybe it sneaks in, you see it, and then steals your shovel, since your shovel is stolen, you can't dig and so you get teleported back up top, and you're like you wake up or something, and then your shovel's there, or maybe he hands it to you. I think we need some kind of cutscene that directs the attention to that moment as well, because right now it just doesn't make any sense. So, with the major or important NPC interactions, let's do some kind of cutscenes so that it is obvious what's happening."

### Digging pace and progression
> "Digging is way too fast, and you can dig straight down to the bottom right from the start. There should be unlock zones for each, digging should be much slower at the start of the game."

> "I think we just need to find a way to make digging more fun as we progress. Give us a reason to want to take vehicles down to the mineshaft."

### Environment
> "I think I'd rather not be able to tear down any of the environment, you have to use the front loader for that. That gives some immediate interest in doing that right away."

### Getting back up
> "There's also a really poor experience when you're trying to get back up out of the mineshaft, so maybe being able to interact better where you're gonna dig would be a good way of handling that, I don't know. We'll have to think through that much better. Let's get creative on how we can go back up when we need to."

### Turning and aim
> "When you're turning, you kind of teleport and jitter around. Like it doesn't know where you are on the screen. It just knows where the pointer is, and so you kind of go on either side of the pointer, so it's very odd. You actually have no idea where you are or where you're digging because your character does not line up with the actual hit box is what it looked like."

### Control hints
> "We also need controls to kind of pop up on the screen when we're in a vehicle, or when we're outside. Have something somewhere on the screen that kind of shows you the button mapping you can use for what you're doing. Like, if you were not standing outside of a vehicle, B would be jump. But if you're standing in front of a vehicle, it would change to enter vehicle."

### The mineshaft elevator
> "The [mineshaft] was also very weak. I think we need to change what button happens there, and when you're standing over the [mineshaft], the action button will come above you and start blinking when it's ready to go down or up. That lets another person come over and if they're within a certain radius, let's go ahead and pull them down as well."
(Voice-to-text wrote "Minecraft"; he means the mineshaft elevator.)
