# The Hill: art direction

Kaden's plan: chunky 2D pixel art. Tiles are 16 px, characters 32 px tall, vehicles 48 to 96 px, buildings are multi-tile sprites with a 3-frame unfold. Filtering is nearest-neighbour and camera zoom stays in whole pixels. Each underground layer has its own dominant hue (dirt brown, clay orange, stone grey-blue, ice pale blue, crystal purple, a warm Dragon cavern), and Hillville is warm and sunny against them. Vehicles are big, round and friendly, with faces in their headlights. Treasures sparkle through the dirt from two tiles away.

- Palette: three proposals, and Kaden picks one (Phase 0 art-direction session, Fable 5.2).
- P1 green `#3cc24a` and P2 red `#e4393c` are for player identity only.
- Original designs only. No real places.
- Jeff and Chuck appear in pixel form wearing hard hats. That 2D look is added back into `openjar-characters`.
